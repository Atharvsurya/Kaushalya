Our Mission

Democratising mentorship across India

The word Kaushalya means skill in Sanskrit. Our platform is built on one conviction: that the fastest way to grow is learning directly from someone who has already walked the path. We connect ambitious learners with proven professionals across tech, design, data, and beyond.

Our Journey

Built with purpose, one step at a time

💡
The Idea

Frustrated by inaccessible mentorship, a team of engineers set out to build a platform where any student could find any expert.

⚙️
The Build

A Spring Boot backend was crafted with JPA, BCrypt security, and a clean REST API. Every endpoint designed around real user flows.

🚀
Launch

Kaushalya went live with mentor registration, student enrollment, admin controls, and a full-featured frontend.

🌱
Growing

The community is expanding. New skills, new mentors, new stories of growth emerge every week.
